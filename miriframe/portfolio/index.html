About Miriframe

Miriframe is a startup freelance graphic design brand founded by Marithes Miraflor, launched in May 2026. It's a young, growing space where design ideas take shape into real visual work.

Right now, Miriframe is still in its early chapter — they're building up a portfolio of hypothetical projects designed to showcase their range and skills in graphic design, from branding concepts to digital layouts, before taking on bigger client work.

Even so, Miriframe is currently open for commissions! Whether you need a logo, poster, social media content, or any custom design, feel free to reach out through the contact section below.

Loved working with Miriframe, or just want to support this new brand? 🌟 Don't forget to leave a review — every bit of feedback helps Miriframe grow!
